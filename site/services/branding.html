Practice 03 · Branding & Marketing

Give your brand a seat at the table.

Strategic positioning and on-the-ground marketing execution — so businesses, retailers and products earn a clear, credible and memorable place in the market.

Talk to branding teamKeep exploring

ROOFY's branding practice helps businesses earn a seat at the table — a position in the market that is clear, credible and memorable. We combine the strategic work (positioning, naming, identity) with the operational work (campaign rollout, content, social) so brands don't just look polished, they actually move.

Most of our brand work runs in tandem with our real-estate and advertising practices — turning a new property project into a brand, or letting an LED campaign work as a beat in a larger story.
