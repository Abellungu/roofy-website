Last updated: 2026-05-12 · (Draft — pending legal review)

This page explains how ROOFY uses cookies on www.roofyinvestments.com. For the full picture of how we collect and use personal information, please read the Privacy Policy.

01 · What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites you visit. They are used to remember preferences, identify sessions and support basic site functionality. A cookie does not by itself collect your name or email, but can be associated with other information you provide.

02 · Cookies we set

We group cookies into two categories — essential (needed for the site to function) and analytics & advertising (set only with your consent). The table below lists the specific cookies currently in use on our site.

03 · How to control cookies

On your first visit, a cookie banner appears. You can choose Accept (all cookies) or Essential only (just the cookies the site needs to run).

You can also delete existing cookies or block new ones via your browser settings. The exact steps differ between browsers — refer to your browser's help documentation.

Note: disabling essential cookies may prevent some functionality from working (e.g. remembering your language choice or that you have dismissed the cookie banner).

04 · Manage your cookie settings

If you change your mind, use the button below to clear your saved cookie choice. The banner will reappear after the page reloads, so you can choose again.
